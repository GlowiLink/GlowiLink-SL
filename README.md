# 🛡️ GlowiLink Privacy Policy
*Last Updated: October 2026*

### 📊 1. DATA COLLECTION & ACCURACY
We collect necessary data (Email, Name, Birthdate, Gender, Bio, and Avatar) to build and authenticate your profile securely via Supabase infrastructure and encrypted auth tokens:
* **Fixed Identity Data:** To maintain platform integrity, your **Name and Birthdate** are permanent once saved. Your **Age** updates automatically on your verified birthday.
* **Gender Policy:** We strictly support and store gender data as either **Male** or **Female**.
* **Other Info:** Your bio and photos remain editable at your discretion.

### 📍 2. LOCATION PRIVACY & DISCRETIONARY SHARING
Your privacy is our priority:
* We use your location to calculate **distance only** and power nearby discovery feeds. 
* You acknowledge that sharing your proximity, exact movements, or coordinates within chats or profiles is entirely at your own discretion. We never sell or display your exact coordinates or residential address to third parties.

### 🔐 3. STORAGE, MEDIA & SECURITY
* **Infrastructure:** All personal data is encrypted and stored securely using **Supabase** infrastructure.
* **Media & Disappearing Messages:** Avatar images and chat photos are securely stored via Supabase storage. View-once images are automatically deleted from server storage immediately after being opened by the recipient. Disappearing messages are purged based on your active chat duration settings.

### 🗑️ 4. PERMANENT DELETION & HIBERNATION
You maintain full control over your data:
* **Hibernate Account:** Hides your profile while preserving your data for easy reactivation upon logging back in.
* **Delete Account:** Triggers an immediate and permanent wipe of your profile, matches, messages, and associated storage files from our servers.

### 🤝 5. THIRD PARTIES & ADVERTISING
* **Data Integrity:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to commercial third parties. Data is only shared when legally mandated or required to protect platform safety.
* **Advertising:** We utilize Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to power our rewarded video system. AdMob may collect non-personal device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve contextually relevant rewarded advertisements.
